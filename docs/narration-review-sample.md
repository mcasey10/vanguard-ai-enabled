## 11. Market-context exclusion-check mechanism

- **Touchpoint:** Fund Selection rationale
- **Segment tone:** A
- **Source data:** VFITX, two different lots in the same fund — one excluded, one not

**Excluded pairing (VFITX / `IRA-VFITX-06`):**
> Selling 3255 of Vanguard Intermediate-Term Treasury Index Fund Admiral Shares from lot IRA-VFITX-06 yields a realized loss of 15 and totals 15 in harvested losses with an impact percentage of 0. This traditional IRA withdrawal of 3255 is taxed as ordinary income regardless of the -15 gain or loss figure, and the sale contributes to rebalancing domestic bonds.

No market-context sentence — `isMarketContextExcluded('VFITX', ['IRA-VFITX-06'], market_context_exclusions)` returns `true`, so `market_context_return_pct` was never included in the figures sent to the model in the first place. Reason on file: *"Real trailing-12-month return (+5.48%) conflicts in sign with this lot's fictional unrealized loss (-$15.00). Adjusting the lot's cost_basis_per_share to resolve the conflict cascades into unrealized_st_gain_loss, total_unrealized_gain_loss, and total_cost_basis at the holding level, three UI pages that render those fields (FundSelectionManual2, FundSelectionManualLot, OrderConfirmation), and computed_aggregates' self-consistency checks... Rather than adjust either the verified real figure or the fictional lot, the market-context narration sentence is suppressed for this specific fund/lot pairing."*

**Non-excluded pairing, same fund, different lot (VFITX / `IRA-VFITX-02`):**
> Selling 27125 from the Vanguard Intermediate-Term Treasury Index Fund Admiral Shares lot acquired on 2014-03-20 reduces domestic_bonds exposure by 27125 while realizing 625 in long-term gain within a traditional IRA. Because this account type is a traditional IRA, the withdrawal is taxed as ordinary income regardless of the gain/loss figure shown, resulting in an estimated gross tax of 0 and an impact percentage of 0.

`isMarketContextExcluded('VFITX', ['IRA-VFITX-02'], ...)` returns `false`; `market_context_return_pct` resolves to the real, sourced `5.48` and was included in the figures — though notably the model's generated text for this particular sample doesn't end up mentioning the market-context figure explicitly, unlike samples #1–#5. Worth a look in the separate review pass: the exclusion mechanism itself is working correctly (confirmed structurally, not just by reading the prose), but the model doesn't consistently narrate every figure it's given.
